# Awesome Tech Salary Resources

A curated list of free interactive calculators, salary benchmarks, and negotiation playbooks for software engineers and tech professionals.

**Focus:** Total compensation, RSU & equity math, sign-on bonus rules, and interview timeline tracking.

## Free Interactive Career & Tax Calculators
*   [Sign-On Bonus Clawback & IRC 1341 Tax Calculator](https://leonstaff.com/tools/sign-on-bonus-clawback-calculator/) - Model pro-rata vs. flat-cliff sign-on repayments, same-year net W-2 vs. cross-year gross paybacks, IRC Section 1341 Claim of Right tax credits, and 2026 CA AB 692 rules.
*   [Interview Silence & Follow-Up Calculator (86 Companies)](https://leonstaff.com/tools/interview-follow-up-calculator/) - Count exact business days elapsed against 86 verified tech & Fortune 500 hiring timelines and generate stage-specific follow-up emails.
*   [W2 vs 1099 Paycheck Calculator](https://www.waveapps.com/payroll/paycheck-calculator) - Take-home paycheck comparison tool.
